## Why This Notice Exists

RTKino was born from a simple conviction: accurate positioning is a tool that should be accessible to everyone — not a luxury controlled by corporations. It was built by a land surveyor, on nights and weekends, over three years of fieldwork, to solve a real problem: the absurd cost of professional GNSS equipment that excludes independent professionals, small firms, farmers, students, and researchers from technology that should serve them.

The same technology that helps a farmer monitor a hillside, a young surveyor start a practice, or a scientist study the earth — can also guide a missile, aim an autonomous weapon, surveil a population, or enable the exploitation of people and land.

The author cannot prevent misuse. Open source means open. But the author can say clearly what this project was made for, and what it was not.

## Intended Use

RTKino was made for people who work with the land and need to know where things are.

- Land surveying and cadastral mapping
- Topographic and geodetic measurement
- Agricultural monitoring and precision farming
- Environmental monitoring, landslide tracking, and geological research
- Construction layout and stakeout
- Drone mapping and photogrammetry ground control
- Education and academic research
- Navigation for civilian and humanitarian purposes
- Any peaceful application that benefits people or communities

## The Author Asks That You Do Not Use RTKino For:

**Weapons and warfare.** This includes integration into weapons systems, guided munitions, autonomous or semi-autonomous weapons platforms, military targeting systems, armed drones, mine-laying equipment, or any device designed to injure or kill human beings. This applies to all parties in all conflicts.

**Mass surveillance.** This includes covert tracking of individuals or populations without their informed consent, and integration into systems designed to monitor, profile, or control civilian populations.

**Human rights violations.** This includes any application that facilitates forced labor, human trafficking, forced displacement, political repression, or the suppression of civil liberties.

**Environmental destruction.** This includes use in activities that cause deliberate, large-scale environmental damage — illegal deforestation, unauthorized mining, dumping of hazardous materials, or destruction of protected ecosystems.

**Exploitation.** This includes any application where the primary purpose is to extract value from people or communities while denying them fair compensation, dignity, or the ability to refuse.

## A Personal Note

I built RTKino because I believe that technology should serve people, not extract from them. I have seen how corporate gatekeeping turns essential tools into mechanisms of exclusion.

The land does not belong to anyone. We are temporary guests. The tools we build to understand it should reflect that humility.

If you use RTKino, I hope you use it to build something good. To measure your field, map your forest, monitor your hillside, survey your neighbor's land for the cost of your time and not a cent more. I hope you teach someone with it, or learn something with it, or solve a problem that was previously locked behind a price tag.

That is what it was made for.
